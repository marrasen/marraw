What to test in this build
==========================

New since the last build you tried. Everything else in README.txt still
applies. Try it on these test photos: deleting and exporting change files.

New in this build
  - Settings (the gear at the foot of the library, or the palette):
    General (crop or fit the grid's pictures), Features (turn the
    culling aids off; their buttons go), Default presets, Cache (size,
    limit, move, clear), Models (delete), Sidecars, and Shared albums
    (the links, with when each was last opened; revoke).
  - Export (Ctrl+E) is marraw's whole dialog: presets (Save as new,
    Update, Rename, Delete), destination with Choose…, a file name
    template with an example, format, quality, resize by long edge,
    colour space, sharpening, watermark, metadata, credit, and a summary.
    A destination that is not there asks before it is made.
  - Copy to clipboard (in Export, Ctrl+Shift+C, or the palette) renders
    the photo and puts the picture on the clipboard. Paste it into
    another program. Please check this one on Windows.
  - Watermarks: Edit… beside Watermark in Export, or the palette. Make
    watermarks of text, a logo (Logo… picks a PNG or JPEG) and bars,
    each placed on a 3 x 3 grid with size, margin and opacity; a bar
    can fade. A frame round the photo, with a deeper bottom if you like.
    The middle shows it over the photo in hand as you change it. Drag
    the elements' rows to reorder them.
  - Select two photos or more in the grid: the bar becomes the
    selection's (count, stars to rate, Pick, Reject, Delete, Adjust,
    "Esc to clear"), and a glass card at the right adjusts them all:
    exposure, contrast and saturation add to each photo's own edit when
    you let go. Paste settings, Restore original, and Apply a preset
    too. Its cross hides it; Adjust brings it back.
  - The library sidebar, as marraw's:
    - Library folders show their shoots; the arrows button sorts them by
      name or date and groups them by year, month or day ("Collapse
      previous years" too). Shoots added by hand sit under the folder
      they share. Headers fold and unfold.
    - Right-click a row for its menu: Open in Cull, Rename (a name in
      the sidebar) or Rename on disk, Locate, Copy path, Share album,
      Include subfolders, Rescan, Render 1:1, Revoke sharing, Hide or
      Remove (the files stay on disk), Move up and down for a block.
    - Filter folders narrows the list. A disconnected drive shows
      Offline. A shared shoot has a green mark.
    - Drag the sidebar's right edge to resize it. The button at its top
      right hides it; the one at the left of the grid's bar brings it
      back.
    - Add folder (at the foot) adds a single shoot or a library folder
      of shoots, browsing the drives and folders with their RAW counts.
      Dropping folders from Explorer on the sidebar adds them too.
  - Share album… (a shoot's menu, or the palette): who can open the link
    (anyone, over Tailscale Funnel, or only your own devices), what they
    can do, download size, and how long it lives. The note at the foot
    says whether this computer can be reached. A link that would publish
    the computer for the first time asks once more before it is made.
    The link is copied when it is made.
  - + and - on a develop control (pick one with its letter, as E for
    Exposure) now show a small pill at the foot of the photo with just
    that control, the panel out of the way. Move the mouse to bring the
    panel back.
  - Visualize spots: while healing (Q), press A, or the switch in
    Retouch. Dust and blemishes stand out as small rings on black.
    Sensitivity sets how keen it is.
  - The pop-out viewer: Ctrl+N (or the palette) opens a second window
    with the photo in hand, following it. Its own zoom and pan: wheel,
    + and -, drag, Z or Space or a double click for fit and 1:1. F11 for
    full screen, P or the pin to keep it on top. Ctrl+N closes it, and
    it opens where it last was.
  - Not here: remote connections.

From the build before
  - The panel's tabs are a bar across it: each an icon over its name.
    The chosen one stands on a pane of brighter glass with a glowing
    line under it; changing tabs, the line runs to the next, stretching
    as it goes, and the pane follows.
  - Presets: after applying a preset, Amount (under the presets) scales
    it from 0% (as before it) to 200% (doubled). Let go, the preset's
    step in History takes the new name, as "Cinematic · 150%". Any other
    edit or another photo ends it.
  - Crop: Auto crops around the subject, with room round it, in the
    shape chosen (Free, 1:1, 3:2 ...). It asks to download the subject
    model the first time. Mirroring turns the picture over at once, as a
    card, while the mirrored frame renders.
  - Masks on the Local tab:
    - Scene and People find the photo's kinds of thing (sky, people,
      foliage ...) or its people, as chips. The pointer over a chip, or
      over the photo, tints the region; a click adds a mask of it, and
      the picking stays armed for more. Escape puts it away. Each asks
      to download its model the first time.
    - Drag a mask's row by the grip at its left to move it up or down
      the list: a mask applies over the ones above it.
    - Up and Down walk the masks' controls, into the next mask past the
      last of one; + and - step the one chosen, Shift further; Escape
      lets it go.
  - Retouch, on the Local tab, under the masks: Heal spots, or Q.
    - Click the photo to heal a spot, drag to make it larger; or choose
      Brush and paint along a blemish. New spots heal, clone or fill, as
      chosen.
    - The chosen spot shows its source, dashed: drag its middle, its
      source or its edge. Click another spot to choose it.
    - Fill asks to download the fill model the first time; Cancel makes
      the spot a heal instead.
    - The list shows each spot; the chosen one's mode, feather and
      opacity under its row. Delete deletes it, 1-9 and 0 set its
      opacity, Escape lets it go, then the tool.
  - Not here: the navigator in the Info tab (the navigator floats over
    the photo instead, clear of the panel).

Fixes and polish
  - A double click zooms in, and the next double click zooms out at
    once, without moving the pointer first.
  - Task progress glides: the bar and its count run smoothly between
    the backend's updates, a download's megabytes too.
  - The panel's buttons, tabs and choices are frosted glass: a pale
    pane with a hairline edge, brighter under the pointer; the chosen
    tab or option a brighter pane. The histogram's ground is glass too.
  - White balance: the eyedropper is the last of the modes. Click it to
    pick; it stays chosen with the white balance it picked. Cancelling
    goes back to the mode before.
  - Detail's Highlights and Demosaic: their names above, the options
    across the whole row, every one readable.

Background tasks
  - Background work shows as chips at the bottom right, above any error
    cards, over the grid and over the photo: a spinner, what it is, how
    far it has got, a bar, and a cross to cancel it. Done, a chip ticks
    and goes. Work that ends within half a second shows nothing.
  - A model download shows its own progress in megabytes: "Downloading
    the subject model 87/170 MB", then the scan's count of photos.
  - Exports and scans no longer post "3 of 20…" notes over the photo:
    the chip shows it.

Errors, glass and the filmstrip
  - The readout at the bottom left no longer shows errors. Moving on
    from a photo while its full resolution loads was never an error and
    now says nothing.
  - Real failures (an export, a preset, Auto, an AI mask, deleting, the
    full resolution) come as cards at the bottom right, over the grid
    and over the photo. They stay until you clear them: the cross on
    each, or Clear all over two or more. The same failure again counts
    up (x2) rather than adding a card.
  - The glass is clearer: the panel, the filmstrip and the other cards
    show more of the photo through them. Their shadows no longer darken
    what shows through.
  - The photo showing in the filmstrip grows half as large again, and
    its neighbours move aside for it.

More of marraw's panel and chrome
  - The panel's header, as marraw's: the photo's name with its flag and
    reject in squares at the right, its stars under it, and its camera
    and exposure. Click them to mark the photo. While the panel is open
    the marks live there; the pill at the top of the photo comes back
    when it shuts.
  - The tabs are marraw's small pill row. The Curve tab has a dot while
    a curve is set.
  - Develop, Curve and Local start with the histogram and the tab's name
    with Undo and Redo buttons.
  - Geometry has a Straighten slider, -15 to +15 degrees, working
    without the crop: the photo turns and its crop shrinks to stay on it.
  - Presets has Clipboard: Copy, Paste and Reset.
  - Info: a tall histogram, the photo's details in mono, then Locate on
    disk, Copy (all the details), Copy folder path and Copy filename.
  - A section's chevron shows only while the pointer is over its heading.
  - After 2.8 seconds with no mouse or keys, everything over the photo
    fades out, but the piece the pointer rests on. Any move brings it
    back. Holding Backspace for the original hides it too.
  - The filmstrip sinks away for the crop and the eyedropper, and their
    bars drop to the foot. The crop's frame fits above its bar.
  - The panel starts 64 pixels from the top, as marraw's.

The panel and the filmstrip float on glass (new)
  - The develop panel (D) is a card of frosted glass at the right, the
    photo showing through it blurred, as marraw's drawer. It slides in
    and out, and slides away for the crop and the eyedropper.
  - Tabs along its top: Develop, Curve, Local (the masks), Presets
    (Auto everything/tone/colour, the presets, History) and Info. Click
    one, or press Tab and Shift+Tab to walk them. The command palette
    (Ctrl+K) has each tab too, and opens the panel for it.
  - The histogram heads Develop, Curve and Local, as in marraw.
  - The stars, flags and navigator keep clear of the panel.
  - The filmstrip at the foot is glass too, centred in the room the panel
    leaves, and as wide as that room: at 4K it holds as many photos as
    fit, not eight. The current photo grows larger, with a ring; the strip
    glides to keep it in the middle. Groups have their times and count
    above them (a group's heading stays at the strip's left edge while
    the group shows), with the time gap on its side between groups, and
    the number of groups at the left. Click a photo to go to it; the
    wheel scrolls the strip.

Masks (with the develop panel open)
  - Masks are on the panel's Local tab, as marraw's: Linear, Radial,
    Brush and Range add a mask; Subject, Background, Depth and Tilt shift
    add an AI mask (the first time asks to download the model).
  - Each mask has a row: click it to choose it or let it go. Its pills:
    Remove (fill what it covers, for brush and AI masks), Invert; then
    show or hide, and delete. The pointer over a row tints the mask red
    on the photo.
  - The chosen mask's settings open under its row: its shape's (feather,
    threshold, focus distance and depth, the range's luminance, hue and
    saturation), its nine tone sliders, and Effects (blur, bokeh, motion
    and zoom blur, glow, light streaks, prism, mosaic, direction).
  - On the photo: a linear gradient's two ends and its line drag; a
    radial's middle, width, height and turn handles drag. While dragging,
    the mask shows tinted.
  - Brush: Paint (or a new brush mask) paints on the photo, with the
    brush's circle in place of the pointer; the eraser erases; Clear
    takes all strokes off; Size, Feather and Flow set the brush.
  - Range: Pick colour, then click the photo: the hue window narrows to
    that colour.
  - Escape stops painting or picking, then lets the mask go.
  - Turning or mirroring in the crop now carries the masks with it.
  - Not yet: Scene and People picking (click a region on the photo),
    moving masks up and down the list, and the keys to walk the masks'
    sliders.

Fixed since build b89595a
  - Cropping no longer shows the cropped edit's sharp tiles over the
    whole frame (the blocks in your screenshot), and holding Backspace no
    longer shows the edit's tiles over the original.
  - The turn buttons in the crop bar now turn the photo on screen at
    once, animated, while the turned frame renders; the crop fades out
    for the turn and back once the pixels come. Mirroring still waits
    for the render.

Crop and straighten (new)
  - With the develop panel open (D), press R, or click "Crop and
    straighten" in the panel's Geometry section. The photo zooms out to show
    its whole frame, with the crop over it: the rest dimmed, thirds, and
    handles at the corners and the sides' middles. The crop's shape and
    size in pixels show in its middle.
  - Drag a corner or a side to resize, or inside to move it. The bar at
    the foot: Free, Original, 1:1, 3:2, 4:3, 16:9 lock the shape. The
    slider straightens, -15 to +15 degrees: the frame turns, and the crop
    shrinks to stay on the photo. Then turn left or right, mirror across
    or upside down, Reset, Done.
  - Enter, Escape, R again or Done finish: the photo renders cropped.
  - Each drag is a step in History; Ctrl+Z undoes it.

Presets (new)
  - A Presets section on the panel's Presets tab. "Creative" holds marraw's
    six (Cinematic, Faded film, Noir B&W, Punchy, Moody, Golden hour);
    "My presets" your own, shared with marraw.
  - Each card shows the photo as the preset would make it. Rest the
    pointer on a card: the photo shows the preset. Click: it is kept, a
    step in History.
  - "Save the edit as a preset…" asks for a name, which groups it carries,
    and whether it adds to a photo's own edit. A cross on your own cards
    deletes them.
  - Ctrl+1 to 9 apply a creative preset by its place, Ctrl+Shift+1 to 9
    one of your own.
  - The pictures render only while the Presets tab shows.

Bursts and culling aids (new)
  - Burst frames have a badge at the top left of their picture: "2/4",
    green on the sharpest frame. Soft photos have an amber mark at the
    bottom right, photos with closed eyes a rose one. The filmstrip shows
    them too, and the photo view's name line says them.
  - Buttons at the right of the filter bar: soft photos only, closed
    eyes only, the sharpest of each burst only; then judge the bursts
    (pick the sharpest of each, reject the rest), look for closed eyes,
    and find the subjects (to judge sharpness on the subject). Looking for
    closed eyes asks to download the eye model the first time.
  - Shift+P picks the frame and rejects the rest of its burst; Shift+X
    only rejects the rest. Ctrl+Z undoes either in one step.

Time-gap groups (new)
  - Sorted by time, the grid splits where more than so many minutes pass
    between frames: each group under a header with its times, how many
    frames, and the gap before it. Choose the gap in the heading, beside
    the size slider ("Group by 6 min gaps", or none). The filmstrip marks
    each gap with a clock between the groups.

Fixed on the way
  - A folder's sort and filters are kept when you leave it and come back
    in the same session.
  - Photos now sort by capture time as soon as the backend has read it,
    and the grid and the photo view always agree on the order.
