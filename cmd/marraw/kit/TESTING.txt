What to test in this build
==========================

New since the last build you tried. Everything else in README.txt still
applies. Try it on these test photos: deleting and exporting change files.

Looks (new in this build)
  - The eyedropper looks like marraw's now: the magnifier shows the
    photo three times as large in place of the pointer, with a faint
    grid, a small target and the pipette at its edge. Beside it a small
    tag reads R G B, and says in red when the spot can't be picked. The
    bar at the foot is glass, with the pipette and a hint, quiet buttons
    and only Done filled.
  - The floating pieces over the photo are frosted glass, as in marraw:
    the note at the top, the stars and flags, the file name box (now a
    card in the corner), the navigator and the eyedropper's bar. They
    blur the photo behind them.

From the last build (e522bc8), if you have not tried them yet
-------------------------------------------------------------

Filter bar (over the grid)
  - Sort is two buttons, Time and Name. The one in use is lit, with an
    arrow for its way. Click it again to turn the order about: the arrow
    turns. Click the other to sort by that.
  - The least rating is five stars: click one to show photos rated that
    or more, click the lit one again to show all. The pointer over them
    lights the ones a click would.
  - Changing the sort or a filter: photos that stay glide to their new
    places, photos that go fade out, photos that come fade in.
  - Switching shoots in the library: the zoom out and in is twice as
    fast as before.
  - A filter that shows nothing says "No photos match the filter".

Stars and flags
  - Stars, the pick flag and the reject cross are icons now, on the
    tiles, in the photo view, in the filmstrip and in the heading's
    counts.
  - Click them, in the grid and in the photo view. A star rates the
    photo; the same star again takes the rating off. The flag picks it,
    the cross rejects it; again takes it off. On a tile, the flag and
    cross show when the pointer is over the tile.
  - Under "Not rejected", reject a photo: it goes from the grid at once,
    as in marraw. Likewise un-picking under "Picks", and a lower rating
    under a star filter. Ctrl+Z brings it back.
  - The same in the photo view: the photo goes and the next one shows.
    The last one gone takes you back to the grid.

White balance
  - The mode buttons (As shot, Auto, Kelvin, Picked) have the whole row,
    with no "Mode" label, and an eyedropper button at the end of it.
  - The eyedropper (W or that button) now works as in marraw:
    - A magnifier follows the pointer over the photo, with the spot's
      R G B beside it. Before you click, it warns in red when a spot
      cannot be picked, and why: "No green light", "Too dark".
    - A click shows the pick, but saves nothing yet. Click other spots
      to compare.
    - A bar at the bottom: As shot, Auto, Reset, Cancel (Escape) and
      Done (Enter). Done keeps the pick as one step in History; Cancel
      goes back to the white balance before.
    - A spot the backend refuses now says why, at the top of the photo.
      On the concert photos most spots are lit by pure blue or red LEDs
      and have no other colour in them: no white balance can make those
      grey. Try the white spotlights, or the Canon photos.
  - Focused on the mode buttons, "?" (Shift and + on a Swedish
    keyboard) shows the shortcuts and leaves the mode alone. Shift and +
    no longer zooms out in the photo view either.

Lens section
  - The concert photos say: No profile for "85mm F1.4 DG HSM | Art 016"
    in the lens database. That is right: the 2016 Sigma 85mm Art has no
    profile in marraw's database, nor in Lensfun's. The Canon photos
    have one. A camera the database does not know says so.
