What to test in this build
==========================

New since the last build you tried. Everything else in README.txt still
applies. Try it on these test photos: deleting and exporting change files.

More of marraw's panel and chrome (new)
  - The panel's header, as marraw's: the photo's name with its flag and
    reject in squares at the right, its stars under it, and its camera
    and exposure. Click them to mark the photo. While the panel is open
    the marks live there; the pill at the top of the photo comes back
    when it shuts.
  - The tabs are marraw's small pill row. The Curve tab has a dot while
    a curve is set.
  - Develop, Curve and Local start with the histogram and the tab's name
    with Undo and Redo buttons.
  - Geometry has a Straighten slider, -15 to +15 degrees, working
    without the crop: the photo turns and its crop shrinks to stay on it.
  - Presets has Clipboard: Copy, Paste and Reset.
  - Info: a tall histogram, the photo's details in mono, then Locate on
    disk, Copy (all the details), Copy folder path and Copy filename.
  - A section's chevron shows only while the pointer is over its heading.
  - After 2.8 seconds with no mouse or keys, everything over the photo
    fades out, but the piece the pointer rests on. Any move brings it
    back. Holding Backspace for the original hides it too.
  - The filmstrip sinks away for the crop and the eyedropper, and their
    bars drop to the foot. The crop's frame fits above its bar.
  - The panel starts 64 pixels from the top, as marraw's.

The panel and the filmstrip float on glass (new)
  - The develop panel (D) is a card of frosted glass at the right, the
    photo showing through it blurred, as marraw's drawer. It slides in
    and out, and slides away for the crop and the eyedropper.
  - Tabs along its top: Develop, Curve, Local (the masks), Presets
    (Auto everything/tone/colour, the presets, History) and Info. Click
    one, or press Tab and Shift+Tab to walk them. The command palette
    (Ctrl+K) has each tab too, and opens the panel for it.
  - The histogram heads Develop, Curve and Local, as in marraw.
  - The stars, flags and navigator keep clear of the panel.
  - The filmstrip at the foot is glass too, centred in the room the panel
    leaves, and as wide as that room: at 4K it holds as many photos as
    fit, not eight. The current photo grows larger, with a ring; the strip
    glides to keep it in the middle. Groups have their times and count
    above them (a group's heading stays at the strip's left edge while
    the group shows), with the time gap on its side between groups, and
    the number of groups at the left. Click a photo to go to it; the
    wheel scrolls the strip.

Masks (with the develop panel open)
  - Masks are on the panel's Local tab, as marraw's: Linear, Radial,
    Brush and Range add a mask; Subject, Background, Depth and Tilt shift
    add an AI mask (the first time asks to download the model).
  - Each mask has a row: click it to choose it or let it go. Its pills:
    Remove (fill what it covers, for brush and AI masks), Invert; then
    show or hide, and delete. The pointer over a row tints the mask red
    on the photo.
  - The chosen mask's settings open under its row: its shape's (feather,
    threshold, focus distance and depth, the range's luminance, hue and
    saturation), its nine tone sliders, and Effects (blur, bokeh, motion
    and zoom blur, glow, light streaks, prism, mosaic, direction).
  - On the photo: a linear gradient's two ends and its line drag; a
    radial's middle, width, height and turn handles drag. While dragging,
    the mask shows tinted.
  - Brush: Paint (or a new brush mask) paints on the photo, with the
    brush's circle in place of the pointer; the eraser erases; Clear
    takes all strokes off; Size, Feather and Flow set the brush.
  - Range: Pick colour, then click the photo: the hue window narrows to
    that colour.
  - Escape stops painting or picking, then lets the mask go.
  - Turning or mirroring in the crop now carries the masks with it.
  - Not yet: Scene and People picking (click a region on the photo),
    moving masks up and down the list, and the keys to walk the masks'
    sliders.

Fixed since build b89595a
  - Cropping no longer shows the cropped edit's sharp tiles over the
    whole frame (the blocks in your screenshot), and holding Backspace no
    longer shows the edit's tiles over the original.
  - The turn buttons in the crop bar now turn the photo on screen at
    once, animated, while the turned frame renders; the crop fades out
    for the turn and back once the pixels come. Mirroring still waits
    for the render.

Crop and straighten (new)
  - With the develop panel open (D), press R, or click "Crop and
    straighten" in the panel's Geometry section. The photo zooms out to show
    its whole frame, with the crop over it: the rest dimmed, thirds, and
    handles at the corners and the sides' middles. The crop's shape and
    size in pixels show in its middle.
  - Drag a corner or a side to resize, or inside to move it. The bar at
    the foot: Free, Original, 1:1, 3:2, 4:3, 16:9 lock the shape. The
    slider straightens, -15 to +15 degrees: the frame turns, and the crop
    shrinks to stay on the photo. Then turn left or right, mirror across
    or upside down, Reset, Done.
  - Enter, Escape, R again or Done finish: the photo renders cropped.
  - Each drag is a step in History; Ctrl+Z undoes it.

Presets (new)
  - A Presets section on the panel's Presets tab. "Creative" holds marraw's
    six (Cinematic, Faded film, Noir B&W, Punchy, Moody, Golden hour);
    "My presets" your own, shared with marraw.
  - Each card shows the photo as the preset would make it. Rest the
    pointer on a card: the photo shows the preset. Click: it is kept, a
    step in History.
  - "Save the edit as a preset…" asks for a name, which groups it carries,
    and whether it adds to a photo's own edit. A cross on your own cards
    deletes them.
  - Ctrl+1 to 9 apply a creative preset by its place, Ctrl+Shift+1 to 9
    one of your own.
  - The pictures render only while the Presets tab shows.

Bursts and culling aids (new)
  - Burst frames have a badge at the top left of their picture: "2/4",
    green on the sharpest frame. Soft photos have an amber mark at the
    bottom right, photos with closed eyes a rose one. The filmstrip shows
    them too, and the photo view's name line says them.
  - Buttons at the right of the filter bar: soft photos only, closed
    eyes only, the sharpest of each burst only; then judge the bursts
    (pick the sharpest of each, reject the rest), look for closed eyes,
    and find the subjects (to judge sharpness on the subject). Looking for
    closed eyes asks to download the eye model the first time.
  - Shift+P picks the frame and rejects the rest of its burst; Shift+X
    only rejects the rest. Ctrl+Z undoes either in one step.

Time-gap groups (new)
  - Sorted by time, the grid splits where more than so many minutes pass
    between frames: each group under a header with its times, how many
    frames, and the gap before it. Choose the gap in the heading, beside
    the size slider ("Group by 6 min gaps", or none). The filmstrip marks
    each gap with a clock between the groups.

Fixed on the way
  - A folder's sort and filters are kept when you leave it and come back
    in the same session.
  - Photos now sort by capture time as soon as the backend has read it,
    and the grid and the photo view always agree on the order.

From earlier builds, if you have not tried them yet
